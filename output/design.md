# Design (Problem 10)

Goal: a collegiate, warm, modern storefront that's easy to shop. Built on the project palette (light blue + desert yellow) with deep Yale navy for type. No new dependencies or web fonts: plain CSS, one inline SVG, and system font stacks. All motion is disabled for visitors with "reduce motion" turned on.

| Change | What I did | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| **Brand system** | A shield monogram (`components/Brand.tsx`) used in the header, footer, chat, and auth pages. A thin navy-and-gold "varsity stripe" under the header and above the footer, and a small navy/gold rule before every section label. One set of color, radius, shadow, and motion tokens in `index.css`. | The site reads as one Yale-flavored shop instead of a template, which builds trust before anyone looks at a price. |
| **Typography & hierarchy** | Serif display face (Iowan/Palatino) for headings and prices, a clean sans for body text, uppercase tracked labels, and a consistent type scale. The hero headline highlights "every corner" with a gold underline. | Clear levels (label → title → detail) make pages scannable, so shoppers find what matters faster. |
| **Navigation** | Navy announcement bar with the shop's address; an animated gold underline on hover and on the current page; a shadow that appears once you scroll; a mobile menu whose icon turns into an × and splits page links from account actions. | Shoppers always know where they are, and the sticky header stays out of the way. |
| **Home page** | Rebuilt as a sequence: hero with a product showcase and a live price tag → four-fact trust strip → image-led category tiles → **"Find your residential college"** chips (built from catalogue tags; each count matches the results it links to) → featured products → a navy **shop assistant band** with sample questions that open the chat pre-filled → visit banner. | It gives several clear ways in (style, college, favorites, ask a person-like assistant), which keeps visitors browsing instead of bouncing. |
| **Product cards** | Photos on a warm sand backdrop with a gentle zoom and a "View details" pill on hover. Clamped name and description. Price and, when a size is chosen, "Only 2 left in M". A **row of six size boxes** with sold-out sizes crossed out. Placeholder descriptions in the data ("Vision blocked…") are replaced by an honest "full description coming soon". | Shoppers can judge price and size availability without clicking. Fewer dead-end clicks and no confusing data text means more confidence. |
| **Product detail** | Large framed image that stays in view (desktop) beside a clear info column: type label, serif title, large price with a **stock pill** ("In stock in 5 of 6 sizes" / "Limited" / "Sold out"), a **colorway** row with color swatches, a bolder size grid, primary and secondary actions, and a short list of honest assurances (live stock, ask the assistant, try it on at 57 Broadway). | Everything needed to decide is in one column, in the order people decide: price, availability, color, size, act. |
| **Search & filters** | One floating filter panel overlapping the page header: search + sort on top, then labeled **Category** and **Your size** rows with count badges. An **active-filters bar** ("Filtered by: Hoodies ×, Size M ×, Clear all"). On phones each chip row becomes a single swipeable line. | Filters are scannable at a glance, and every applied filter is visible and removable in one tap. |
| **Login / Create account** | Split layout: a navy brand panel with the crest and what an account gives you (saved assistant chats, a personal greeting) beside the form card. Larger inputs with a soft focus ring and clear inline errors. On phones the form comes first. | Account pages feel like part of the store, and the benefits explain why to sign up. |
| **Chat as a storefront feature** | Launcher is a navy pill with the gold crest and "Ask us · Sizes, stock & gifts". The panel has a navy header with the crest, a green "online" dot, and "Shop assistant · checks live stock". Warm bubbles on a subtle dotted paper background, gold typing dots, a gold "Asking about" strip on product pages, and a near full-screen sheet on phones. The assistant is also promoted on Home, in the footer, and on product pages. Small fixes: the generic starter suggestions are hidden on product pages (the product questions replace them), and asking about the product you're already viewing no longer pushes a duplicate card panel onto the page. | It looks and sounds like the shop's own help counter rather than a bolted-on widget, so shoppers are more likely to ask (and get unstuck) instead of leaving. |
| **Motion** | Short, eased transitions only: card lift and image zoom, nav underline, chat panel rising in, bubbles fading up, results panel easing in, button lift. Everything turns off under `prefers-reduced-motion`. | Feedback feels responsive and polished without distracting or hurting accessibility or performance. |

## Reviewed

Checked in the running site at desktop width and at 375 × 812 (mobile): Home, Products (with filters), a product detail page, About, Log in / Create account, the mobile menu, and the chat (desktop panel and mobile sheet, including a live reply with a product card). No horizontal overflow on mobile (page width 375 = viewport), no console errors, and a clean production build.

Regression pass in the new design: log in → personal greeting and 10 restored chat messages; chat search "Show me your jackets" → 8 cards on the page; clicking a card opens its detail page; "Your size: XL" → 77 products; college chip "Benjamin Franklin (3)" → 3 results.
